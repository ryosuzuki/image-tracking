Interpretation rules

  • Camera-overlay web AR is not automatically immersive spatial WebXR.
  • Swift ARKit on Quest is N/A. Unity iPhone requires a separate signed iOS build and physical test.
  • WebXR must actually expose raw passthrough pixels aligned to an XRView. Immersive session support alone is insufficient.
  • Only manually identified camera-and-marker stationary intervals produce centroid jitter. Pixel jitter is not millimetre accuracy.
  • Fit residual is not held-out physical registration error. Independent millimetre error is empty unless explicitly measured and supplied.
  • Detection fractions from different lighting, distance, tilt, card sizes or protocols are not a controlled ranking.
  • Monitor pages, printed pages, accessibility inputs, real touch and real controllers remain distinct evidence conditions.
