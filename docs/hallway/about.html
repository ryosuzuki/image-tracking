One shared marker. One venue coordinate system.

Scan the venue QR, open the camera, and align to the single shared80mm ArUco ID0. The marker establishes the common coordinate frame. Each paper’s position is stored in that frame. After alignment, the SLAM engine tracks the phone so you can look away from the marker and explore the registered papers.

This is not per-paper marker tracking. Losing sight of the marker does not hide papers. Losing spatial tracking does hide them and requires alignment again; a stale camera pose is never presented as a recovered room.

Installation and venue setup

The physical paper layout must first be registered by the venue operator. No hallway coordinates are fabricated from the reference movie. The current deployment includes no surveyed hallway layout. Until one is installed, the browser gallery works but spatial paper overlays are not positioned.

Staff can use venue setup: align to the common marker, choose a paper and tap its center on the marker’s wall. Export the venue.json and install it with the static site. The setup tap tool assumes papers on that same plane. Other walls require measured positions and orientations in the same common frame, using the documented venue schema. Local drafts are not shared with visitors until published.

Verification boundary

Shared-frame math, loss/reset behavior and browser integration are tested separately from physical tracking. This is not a claim of verified iPhone/Android hallway SLAM, drift, scale or accuracy. Actual phone translation/rotation, look-away/return, motion permissions and venue fit remain physical acceptance checks. The engine runs locally; camera images are not uploaded by this application.
