# Storage-grounded lecture expansion — 12 October 2026

This edition replaces a narrow 18-example starting collection with a Storage-grounded lecture atlas. **49 examples, 47 image previews and 13 video links** are now included, including 34 locally rendered published-paper figure excerpts. It preserves the native APK, printable kit and original implementation lanes.

## What was actually searched

- **8,100 accessible PDF paths** enumerated across the existing Storage roots. Every enumerated path was reconciled to exactly one extraction outcome.
- **6,535 distinct SHA-256 hashes**; **1,565 duplicate path aliases**. Different-byte versions remain separate during retrieval and are merged at paper identity only after checking DOI/title/version.
- **7,652 text-readable files**, **446 sparse/scanned files**, **one extraction error**, and **one encrypted file**.
- **116,102 PDF pages** in the extraction-attempt inventory. Repeated versions/aliases are included; this is not a count of unique pages read by a person.
- Sparse/encrypted records collapsed to **328 unique hashes**. Local first-page OCR was attempted for all: **244 returned text**, **83 returned no text**, and **one was encrypted**. First-page OCR is a discovery screen, not full-paper reading.
- Protected system Trash/temporary directories were inaccessible and are explicit inventory exceptions. No original PDF, private project or unrelated record was moved, rewritten, or uploaded.

The private manifest records each file's actual path, hash, page count, extraction state, page-local hits and duplicate aliases. Public metadata intentionally does not expose private filenames, administrative documents, manuscripts or research-library paths.

## Retrieval is not inclusion

Broad all-page searches returned **1,749 unique hash-level keyword candidates**. A separately frozen vocabulary requery found **459 additional keyword candidates**. These are not 2,208 accepted papers: they include versions, citation-only mentions, unrelated AR, grant/CV material, and private drafts. Such records are not added to the public atlas simply to increase its size.

Only source-verified examples with substantive mechanism evidence are promoted. Full-paper entries are read beyond abstract through method, findings/limitations and references. PaperTrail, PaperToPlace and RealitySummary explicitly record targeted method/evaluation/discussion section reading rather than a full-paper reading claim. One scanned chess-interface paper was recovered and read through all 11 pages using local OCR. Individual cards identify their sensing method and distinguish continuous pose tracking, recognition, pen coordinates, projection, authoring and simulated interaction.

## Independent omission audit

Candidate retrieval and the missing-paper adversary froze separate plans before comparing results. The adversary used separate local extraction, venue/program checks, actual seed reference sections and author/publication records.

Its eight independently read local recovery seeds were all found in the completed candidate set. This does **not** prove complete recall. Generalized repair classes include:

- Template/default PDF titles such as “SIGCHI Conference Paper Format” hiding real papers.
- Paper animation and physical authoring indexed under graphics/TEI rather than image tracking.
- Hybrid pen/document/projection systems using different sensing vocabulary.
- Review scope exclusions: a paper–digital review's 60 studies are not a ceiling for this lecture's broader methods/animation/projection question.
- DOI/title/version duplicates and citation-only false local presence.

**MagicBook corrected a filename-search false absence:** the actual local paper was present under a misspelled filename, and its full text and figure are now checked. A filename-only negative result is not sufficient.

**PaperMech is a concrete false-presence trap:** a local PDF mentions it, but that PDF's actual title is Canvas3D. A citation or body mention does not establish the PaperMech paper is locally available. The same rule applies to remembered Clement Zheng/Ellen Do animation works and newer program-only candidates.

## Remaining gaps and stopping status

**Corpus status: `requery-required` — local inventory/extraction complete; literature saturation not reached.**

The requery produced new candidates, not two consecutive zero-eligible independent rounds. Forward citation closure, the unscreened broad candidate tail, image-only text gaps and exact remembered project identities are not silently declared complete. The coverage report records this boundary rather than equating a file sweep with reading every paper or finding every relevant work.

Unresolved source identities/media include the exact Pokémon-card app, the remembered bird animation, “Magic Ink,” and the precise Ishii storytelling project. Candidate titles such as Cartoonimator, Rig-a-Doodle, ReactFold, Paper Playground, Printed Light Tags and PaperMech require their own verified full texts/figures before inclusion. Venue-program metadata alone is not proof of identity, local presence or implemented sensing.

## Visual provenance

Source-first author/project images are preferred. When needed, the atlas uses limited, attributed excerpts of figures from actual published papers, preserving complete panels and labels. The manifest binds extracted images to PDF SHA-256, figure/page, crop coordinates, rendering method and image hash. Original authors retain their rights; these are not newly generated illustrations or redistributed whole papers. Private drafts and room/capture images are excluded.

Videos are linked from canonical author/project sources. Playback verification is stated separately; a source link is not a claim that every clip was watched.

## Reproducibility and scope

The controller reconciled the original 8,100-path inventory against the outcome manifest. Included public metadata, figure provenance and source URLs are supplied in [the media manifest](lecture-media.json). Exact private inventory/reading/search/OCR traces are retained separately for the owner. The public source remains English; this is a lecture review workbench, not a finished slide deck or a systematic-review publication.
